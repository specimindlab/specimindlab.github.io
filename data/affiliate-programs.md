# Affiliate programs: what to join, how, and how fast

Researched 2026-10-10/11 from each tool's own affiliate page and terms first (third-party sources marked as such). Rates change often: check the dashboard before quoting a number anywhere. Not legal or tax advice.

## The short version

- **Of the 65 calendar tools we checked, about 40 run an open affiliate program.** The best for us pay a share of every payment for 12 months or more (Fliki, Taskade, Postiz, Murf, ElevenLabs, Tripo, Meshy, Synthesia, Colossyan, OpusClip, Gamma). Some pay once (Adobe 85% of the first month, Lovable up to $100, Descript $25).
- **About 15 programs let us in the same day** (automatic or self-serve sign-up). Most others review by hand in **1 to 10 business days**. A few want followers first (HeyGen 5K, Pictory's ambassador track 10K, Luma's creator program 5K).
- **Nothing guarantees approval**, but four things remove almost every rejection reason: a live site with real reviews (we have it), a contact email on that site (**we don't, yet**), the same name and verified socials everywhere, and saying plainly we promote only with organic video and our review site.
- **Apply before we film a tool, not after.** The "Not sponsored" or "Affiliate link" label is burned into the video. If a tool approves us after its video is out, that video stays "Not sponsored" forever.
- Verdicts, scores and catches never change for a partner. That is in every application below, because it is why viewers will trust the "Worth it" links.

## Do these first (one evening)

1. **Contact email on the hub.** Almost every reviewer checks for one. Make one address just for SPECIMIND (e.g. a new Gmail, or hello@ a custom domain later) and give it to Claude: it goes on the About page and every application. *A custom domain with a matching email (specimindlab.com + hello@specimindlab.com, about $10/year) is the strongest version: PartnerStack and Dub both flag an email that doesn't match the site.*
2. **Same name everywhere:** SPECIMIND / @specimindlab on YouTube, Instagram, X, the hub and each network profile. A small SPECIMIND LinkedIn page helps PartnerStack match the account. Don't use a VPN while applying (a country mismatch is a fraud flag).
3. **Get paid in India:** a PayPal India account (verified PAN, Indian bank account; PayPal asks for a purpose code on each payment) and a Payoneer or Wise account as backup. Keep every FIRA/FIRC (proof of foreign payment).
4. **W-8BEN** (the US form for a non-US person; use your PAN as the foreign tax ID). Impact won't release money until it's approved. It doesn't mean you owe US tax.
5. **Join the two big networks now; they gate dozens of programs:**
   - **PartnerStack Network** ([partnerstack.com](https://partnerstack.com) → Become a partner): reviewed "in most cases within 5 business days"; median 2 days from approval to the first program. You can apply to programs while you wait; they're held and sent automatically. Pays out by PayPal or Airwallex direct deposit to an Indian bank ($5 minimum).
   - **Impact.com** marketplace ([impact.com](https://impact.com) → Partners → Sign up): reviewed "within 72 hours". Pick **USD as the account currency** (it can't be changed later, and PayPal withdrawals don't work with INR); INR bank wire is also supported. $10 minimum.
   - Dub, Rewardful, FirstPromoter, Tolt, PromoteKit, Lemon Squeezy and Partnero programs are joined one by one from each tool's page; many approve instantly.

## What makes approval quick and close to certain

The common rejection reasons, and our fix for each:

| Rejection reason | Our fix |
|---|---|
| Thin or dead website | Hub with 11 review pages (6 tested and scored), a ranking page and "How we test" with our disclosure policy. Point every application at the most similar review. |
| No contact / identity mismatch | Contact email on the hub (to do); same name everywhere; no VPN; one account per network |
| Social accounts not verified | Connect and verify YouTube, Instagram and X in every network profile (Dub says verified profiles are reviewed faster) |
| Few followers | Lead with the site and the testing method; start with the same-day programs; reapply after 60-90 days of posting |
| Vague description | Use the blurb below |
| Promotion methods brands ban | Tick only content/review/social video. Say: no paid search, no brand bidding, no coupon sites, no cashback |
| Wrong niche | Apply only to tools we've tested or scheduled |
| Country | Say our audience is English-speaking and global, India-based; ask the partner manager directly if a program is region-limited (Granola rejects untargeted regions, CapCut pays only US/UK/DE/FR referrals) |
| Fraud flags | Never buy through our own link; never offer cashback |

**Then email the partner manager** the same day you apply (PartnerStack has a Messages tab even while pending; on Impact, message the brand). A short note with a link to our review of a similar tool and the date we'll test theirs works better than waiting. If declined, ask politely why, and ask for a direct invite link: PartnerStack join links and Impact brand invites skip the marketplace review.

## Disclosure (affects approval and payouts)

- **On screen:** the whole-video label beats ASCI's rule (one-third of a 15 s-2 min video) and the FTC's. The FTC warns some people don't know "affiliate link" means we get paid, so the description line next to the link should say it: *"Affiliate link: we may earn a commission. It never changes our score."* (`/affiliate` writes it into the video copy.)
- **YouTube:** tick "Includes paid promotion" in Studio on videos where we hold an affiliate link or got a free plan for that tool.
- **Instagram:** "Paid partnership" needs the brand's permission in the branded-content tool; use it when a brand gave us something and agrees. Our own label stays either way.
- **X:** turn on "content disclosure" (the Paid Partnership label, since March 2026) on affiliate videos.
- Free credits or free plans from a brand count as a material connection under ASCI even if we weren't asked to post.

## Getting paid in India (high level)

- PartnerStack → PayPal (2% fee, max $20) or Airwallex to an Indian bank ($4 + 1%). Impact → wire in INR or PayPal (USD account currency). Dub → Stripe Express to an Indian bank (free, up to 15 business days). Rewardful programs → the brand pays by PayPal or Wise. Tolt → PayPal, Wise, local bank.
- Affiliate income is taxable in India; GST registration is generally needed above ₹20 lakh a year, and since 30 March 2026 commission earned from overseas companies can count as a zero-rated export. **Talk to a CA before the first big payout**, especially on which tax rules apply to commission income.
