Midjourney makes images from your words. It has no free plan, so we didn't test it and we don't give it a score. Here's what your money gets you, from their own pages.

The cheapest plan is $10 a month. That buys about 800 images, roughly 1.3 cents each.
The catch: on the $10 and $30 plans, everyone can see the images you make. Keeping them private starts at $60 a month.

Good for: mood boards you don't mind sharing. Not for: client work that has to stay private.
