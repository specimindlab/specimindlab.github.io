Liquid AI's d1 is a small, free AI that answers questions about pictures. Its free demo page has a game called Quick Draw: you draw a word, and it guesses while you draw, out of 16 possible words. We played one round (6 words), drawing our own simple line drawings stroke by stroke, no account.
