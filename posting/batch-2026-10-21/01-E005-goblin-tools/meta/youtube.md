## Description
Not sponsored. No affiliate link, and Goblin Tools didn't pay for this.

Goblin Tools is a free website that breaks a chore into small steps. We typed one chore, "clean the garage", into its Magic ToDo and tried three spice levels (how far it breaks things down), one try each, no account.

Level 1: 6 steps in about a second. Level 3: 11 useful steps (sort into keep, donate or trash, sweep, set up shelves). Level 5: 33 steps, and it drifts from cleaning into repairs: patch the walls, repaint, oil the garage door.

Our score: 94/100 (quality 44/50, speed 15/15, free plan 25/25, price 10/10). Our best score so far.
Good for: chores you keep putting off. Not for: level 5 if you want a short list.
Tip: start at level 2 or 3. Level 5 adds jobs you never asked for.

Free: everything on the website, no account, no limit published. Paid: $3 a month (Goblin Tools Pro saves and syncs your lists and lets you share them).
