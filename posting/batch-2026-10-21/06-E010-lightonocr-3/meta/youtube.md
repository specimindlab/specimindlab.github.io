## Description
Not sponsored. No affiliate link, and LightOn didn't pay for this.

LightOnOCR-3 is a new free AI from LightOn that turns photos of pages into text. We gave its free demo one photo of a handwritten note (46 words, on the back of an old photo card), one try, no account, and checked it against our own word-by-word reading.

It read 42 of 46 words in about 4 seconds. But three mistakes change the story: "Mum" became "My own", the cat Butch became "Butel", and the last line, "Ginger Tom", became "thing. Love".

Our score: 54/100 (quality 20/50, speed 15/15, free plan 16/25, price 3/10: no price published). Skip it for handwriting you need word for word.
Good for: a quick first draft you'll check. Not for: when you need every word right.
Tip: always read OCR against the original, especially names and the last line.

Free: the demo page, no account; the model is free to download (Apache 2.0). Paid: not published.
