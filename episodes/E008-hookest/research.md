Calendar row R024 planned Hookest as a Rare Sighting with "Free tier (verify)". Verified: the hook generator is free with no signup, so the row moved to Free Range (a free plan test) and the batch keeps two little-known-tool videos apart.

## What it is
- Hookest is "a free hook video library where creators download transitional hook MP4 clips" for Instagram, TikTok and YouTube. [https://hookest.com/, accessed 2026-10-10]
- Its free YouTube Shorts Hook Generator "writes attention-grabbing opening lines for the first three seconds of a video"; the page says "No signup · 100% free · Ready in seconds", lets you pick a style (Curiosity, POV, Storytime, Tutorial, Bold Claim, Question, Statement, Statistic), a tone, a language and 3, 5 or 6 hooks. Each hook shows its word count and spoken length; "Green lands inside the 3-second hook window." [https://hookest.com/free-youtube-shorts-hook-generator, accessed 2026-10-10]

## Free / paid
- Free plan: $0, "No card needed"; lists the hook generators as free tools, no usage cap published. [https://hookest.com/pricing, accessed 2026-10-10]
- Pro: "$9/month", "Cancel anytime". The pricing page says "Pro adds competitor listening and the MCP server" but lists both under Free too, so what Pro unlocks is unclear. [https://hookest.com/pricing, accessed 2026-10-10] On screen: only "Pro: $9 a month".

## Launch

## Our test
- One generation with the page defaults (style Curiosity, tone Casual, English) and 5 hooks, describing our own Goblin Tools test (E005). [raw/auto/run.json]
- We rated the hooks by our own hook rules (prompts/voice.md, CLAUDE.md): a number or a concrete outcome, true to the test, no hype words. Usable: #1 ("What if cleaning your garage had 33 steps instead of one?") and #5 ("This AI turned one task into dozens"). Not usable: #2 and #4 (vague, no outcome), #3 ("You won't believe...", clickbait). Hookest's own checker marked #1 and #2 as running long (4.2 s and 4.6 s for a 3-second window).
