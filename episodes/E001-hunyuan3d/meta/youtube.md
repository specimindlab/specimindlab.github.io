Hunyuan3D: free AI turned 3 lamps into 3D (71/100)

## Description
Not sponsored. No affiliate link, and Tencent didn't pay for this.

Hunyuan3D is a free AI from Tencent: you give it a photo, it gives you a 3D model. We gave it 3 desk lamp photos on its free demo page, one try each, no account.

Lamp 1 was shiny metal, and it fell apart into 6 pieces. Lamp 2 worked, but it kept the table too. Lamp 3 came out clean, in one piece. Each took about 20 seconds.

Our score: 71/100 (quality 28/50, speed 15/15, free plan 18/25, price 10/10).
Good for: quick 3D shapes of matte things. Not for: anything shiny or metal.
Tip: shiny surfaces confuse it, so photograph matte objects on a plain background.

Free: about 6 models a day with no account (grey shapes only). Colours need an account. Paid: $9 a month (Hugging Face's paid plan).
All the scores and our 3D ranking: specimindlab.github.io/001 (link in our channel profile)
