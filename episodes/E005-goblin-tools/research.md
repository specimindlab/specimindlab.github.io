# E005 · Goblin Tools (Magic ToDo) · research (accessed 2026-10-10)

Nothing contradicts the calendar row: the website is free with no account, as planned.

## What it is
- Goblin Tools is a collection of "small, simple, single-task tools, mostly designed to help neurodivergent people with tasks they find overwhelming or difficult". [https://goblin.tools/About, accessed 2026-10-10]
- Made by Bram De Buyser, "an AI, software and data engineer". [https://goblin.tools/About, accessed 2026-10-10]
- Magic ToDo breaks a to-do item into steps; a "spiciness" control (1 to 5 chilli peppers) sets how far it breaks things down. Seen on the page itself during our test. [https://goblin.tools/ToDo, accessed 2026-10-10]
- Most tools use AI models "from different providers, both open and closed source"; the site warns results are guesswork. [https://goblin.tools/About, accessed 2026-10-10]

## Free plan
- "Most of the site is free" and "will stay free without ads or paywalls"; "existing features will never be locked behind a paywall". No usage limit is published. [https://goblin.tools/About, accessed 2026-10-10]
- No account needed: our test ran in a fresh browser with no sign-in (accounts exist but are optional, for history and sync). [our test, raw/auto/runs.json; https://goblin.tools/About, accessed 2026-10-10]

## Paid
- Goblin Tools Pro: "$3 / €3 / £3 per month", 1-week free trial, cancel any time. Adds synced Magic ToDo lists, the last 50 results per tool, saved templates, custom Formalizer tones, standing instructions and list sharing. "Everything else in Goblin Tools stays free for everyone." [https://goblin.tools/pro/about, accessed 2026-10-10]
- Mobile apps: "a low price" on Android and iOS (no price on the site). [https://goblin.tools/About, accessed 2026-10-10] A third-party price tracker lists the Android app at US$3.49 one-time. [https://apppricinglab.com/app/google_play/com.goblintools, accessed 2026-10-10] Not shown on screen.

## Where it runs / what it takes
- Habitat: web browser; Android and iOS apps. [https://goblin.tools/About, accessed 2026-10-10]
- Feeds on: a line of text (a task); voice input button on the page.

## Launch
- Launch date: not published on the site. [https://goblin.tools/About, accessed 2026-10-10] A 2023 blog post already describes it. [https://juliebestry.com/2023/11/06/3-simple-but-powerful-productivity-resources-right-in-your-browser-tab/, accessed 2026-10-10]

## Known limitations reported by users
- Without an account nothing is saved between visits. [https://blog.saner.ai/goblin-tools-review/, accessed 2026-10-10]
- Output quality varies because general-purpose AI models do the work; the site itself says to treat results as guesswork. [https://goblin.tools/About, accessed 2026-10-10]
