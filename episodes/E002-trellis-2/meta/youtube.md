## Description
Not sponsored. No affiliate link. TRELLIS 2 is a free, open-source AI from Microsoft and nobody paid for this test.

You give it one picture, it gives you a 3D model. We gave it one cartoon dragon drawing on its free demo page, one try, no account. 41 seconds later we had a full 3D dragon, with the wings, the horns and the claws.

The catch: from the side, which the drawing never showed, the tail is flat like a paddle. And without an account you can look at the model but you can't download it.

Our score: 72/100 (quality 35/50, speed 12/15, free plan 15/25, price 10/10). That's our best 3D tool so far, one point above Hunyuan3D.
Good for: turning sketches into 3D drafts. Not for: when you need to download the file for free.
Tip: cut the background out of your drawing before you upload it.
