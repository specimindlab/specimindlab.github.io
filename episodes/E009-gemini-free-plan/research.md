# E009 · Free Gemini changed on 9 Oct · research (accessed 2026-10-10)

Research only (desk study). Calendar row R042 said "From 9 Oct 2026 free users move to Flash-Lite". Google's own help article is more precise than that and is what the video follows.

## What changed (Google's help article)
- Title: "Changes to Gemini model access and limits". Personal accounts without a Google AI plan are affected from 9 October 2026. [https://support.google.com/gemini/answer/17004136?hl=en, accessed 2026-10-10]
- Without a plan, "Auto" becomes the default: each prompt goes to the model it suits best, so most go to Flash-Lite and harder ones may go to Flash or Pro. "If smart model selection is turned off, chats for users without an AI plan will use Flash-Lite for all responses." [same, accessed 2026-10-10]
- Every model gets low, medium and high effort; higher effort uses more of your limit. Limits are compute-based, refresh every 5 hours until a weekly limit; Pro, media generation, Deep Research and extended thinking use more. [same, accessed 2026-10-10]
- Compute-based limits began 17 May 2026 (users over 18). [same, accessed 2026-10-10]
- AI Plus subscribers get an email with their date; Plus limits are 2x standard, Pro 4x. [same, accessed 2026-10-10]

## Plans and prices (US)
- AI Plus: $4.99 a month; keeps Flash-Lite and Flash, loses Pro. AI Pro: $19.99 a month; all three models and gains Deep Think. AI Ultra: $99.99 and $199.99 a month. Reported 3 Oct 2026. [https://9to5google.com/2026/10/03/gemini-model-limits-oct-26/, accessed 2026-10-10]
- Same summary, with the help-article link: [https://www.notebookcheck.net/Google-Gemini-drops-Flash-and-Pro-for-free-users-on-October-9.1415964.0.html, accessed 2026-10-10]

## What the headlines got wrong
- Many reports said free users "only get Flash-Lite". Google's article says Auto may still send harder prompts to Flash or Pro; only choosing a model yourself is limited to Flash-Lite. [support.google.com article above vs. https://www.technology.org/2026/10/07/gemini-free-users-flash-lite-only-october-9/, accessed 2026-10-10]
