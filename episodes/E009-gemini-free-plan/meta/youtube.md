## Title (40 characters)
Free Gemini changed Oct 9: what you lost

## Description
Not sponsored. No affiliate links. Research only: we read Google's own help page, so there's no score.

From 9 October 2026, free Gemini accounts use "Auto": Gemini picks the model for each question. Most answers come from the smallest model (Flash-Lite); harder questions may still go to Flash or Pro. What you lost: choosing a model yourself. Turn Auto off and every answer comes from the small model.

Many headlines said free users "only get Flash-Lite". Google's own page is more careful than that.

Your three choices now:
1. Stay free: Auto, mostly the small model, Pro sometimes for hard questions.
2. Google AI Plus, $4.99 a month (US): the middle model, but it loses Pro too.
3. Google AI Pro, $19.99 a month (US): every model, plus Deep Think.

Limits now refresh every 5 hours up to a weekly cap, and Pro, images and video use them up faster.
Lesson: read the company's own page before the headlines.
