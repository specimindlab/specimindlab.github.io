OpenAI's Sora lasted 7 months. What to use now

## Description
Not sponsored. No affiliate links. Research only: Sora is shut down, so there was nothing left to test, and we give it no score.

OpenAI's Sora app launched on 30 Sep 2025 and closed on 26 Apr 2026, just under 7 months later. The last way in, OpenAI's developer access, closed on 24 Sep 2026. OpenAI says it deletes Sora data after the shutdown, so videos people didn't export are gone.

Three places to make AI videos now:
1. Wan (Alibaba): open source and free to download. Its free demo page wasn't working when we checked on 10 Oct.
2. Kling (Kuaishou): an app like Sora was, with free credits once you make an account (Kling doesn't publish how many).
3. Gemini Omni (Google): replaced Veo 3.1 in the Gemini app; paid plans only.

Lesson: keep your own copies of anything you make on an AI app. When it shuts down, your files can go with it.
